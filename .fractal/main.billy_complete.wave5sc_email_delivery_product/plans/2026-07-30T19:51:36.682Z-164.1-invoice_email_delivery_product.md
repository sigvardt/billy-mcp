   frozen invoice email and invoice-delivery POSTs. Use strict, flat FastMCP
   inputs, ticket-only executes, exact canonical bindings, redacted errors, and
   fail-closed provisional response mappers.
2. Register the four tools in the root server and create an offline contract
   suite covering schemas, request wires, zero-HTTP previews, every ticket
   rejection class, GLN validation, redaction, malformed envelopes, URL lock,
   and one-attempt execution.
3. Update the authoritative coverage evidence and root-registry expectations
   required to regenerate the two special rows; regenerate only derived coverage
   artifacts and keep live, UI, vision, bulk, and overall completeness red.
4. Run the focused suite and full non-live quality gates, record the delivered
   offline limitation in node memory, then commit and report the exact evidence
   for the parent’s required post-merge Grok product review.
