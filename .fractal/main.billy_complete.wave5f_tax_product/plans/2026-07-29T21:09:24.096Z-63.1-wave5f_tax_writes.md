# 63.1 Wave-5f tax ticketed writes

## Goal

Deliver the frozen offline-only Wave-5f tax CUD cohort: twelve ticketed
FastMCP tools for `taxRates` and `taxRateDeductionComponents`, root
registration at 190 `api_*` tools, and six regenerated offline coverage rows.
Keep live, UI, vision, bulk, special-route, and completeness claims unchanged.

## Constraints and approach

- This is a leaf (`max_depth=max_children=max_descendants=0`), so no delegation
  is possible; implement and verify the bounded slice locally.
- Treat the accepted Wave-5f contract and independent freeze review as binding.
  Reuse the server-owned confirmation store and `WriteProtocolService`; do not
  conduct research or make network calls.
- Clone the established catalog/bill write pattern while retaining the exact
  tax roots: parent `taxRate`/`taxRates` with optional
  `taxRateDeductionComponents`, and child
  `taxRateDeductionComponent`/`taxRateDeductionComponents` with no parent root.
