   the failure. Then implement.
2. **Exact visible text, not substring role names.** Click a button only when
   its trimmed inner text equals the label. Never use `text=Ret` or string
   `name="Ret"`. Keep regex `^Gem$` for save.
3. **One Ret click.** After `input[name=name]` is visible, do not click Ret
   again.
4. **Delete first.** `2752BF9A` forbids a new disposable record until leftovers
   are gone through FastMCP and a fresh session proves absence.
5. **Live qualification uses `create_server()` `call_tool`.** Vision stays
   `pending_review`. No greening.
