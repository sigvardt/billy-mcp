## Design choices

1. **TDD first (`728BD2E4`).** Confirm honesty-16 still length 16
   and red. First product edit is a failing unit fixture. Current
   control dump flags (`click_open`, `action_option_role_count=0`,
   no post-click keys) fail `kunde_post_click_missing_keys`. Run
   it. Capture the failure. Then implement. Do not reuse
   `REQUIRED_KUNDE_CONTROL_KEYS` as this fixture.
2. **Honesty-16 stays red.** Do not remap invoice tools.
3. **Tagged customer first.** `ui_clients_create_preview` +
   execute one `MCP-UI-INV-` + 8 hex name. Confirm that exact
   name in a fresh interface session before the invoice form.
4. **One instrumented click.** After baseline, click the proved
   `pickerfield` wrapper once. Do not type. Do not click the
   overlay DIV. Do not send a second diagnostic click.
5. **Scoped observer.** Attach `MutationObserver` only to the
   contact input, the `pickerfield` wrapper, and any initially
   hidden linked subtree. Do not scan the rest of the page.
6. **Role option is not enough.** Pass or fail on allowlisted
   changed-node fields, not `get_by_role("option").count()`.
7. **Exact match.** `exact_match_target` is true only when
   exactly one changed node is visible, interactive, and
   (`text_exact_match` or `ax_name_exact_match`). Hash text and
   AX names. Never persist the raw strings.
8. **If one exact match.** Add a focused failing fixture for that
   node, then the smallest selection click. Continue the invoice
   draft through preview / execute and independent fresh-session
   UI read-back only if line, date, and **Gem som kladde** are
   already safely proved. Else stop at `UI_CHANGED`.
9. **Cleanup.** Delete the customer in reverse. Prove absence in
   a fresh session. Purge raw DOM/AX under
   `~/.local/share/billy-mcp/`. Commit allowlisted summary only.
10. **Vision.** Live test may write `author=live_test` and
    `reviewer_verdict=pending_review` only.
11. **No remap.** Preview `tool_name` waits for a later slice
    after independent accept + purge.
