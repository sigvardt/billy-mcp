## Adopted execution decision

This plan adopts the already-completed research handoff rather than replacing
it. The research artefacts are uncommitted but clean under `git diff --check`:
the durable state and shared probe policy have been updated, while the raw
official-document snapshot and probes remain ignored in node scratch. The
current official-document fingerprint matches the frozen inventory, so the next
deliverable is a reviewed Wave-5h contract, not another inventory rewrite.

Wave-5h is deliberately limited to three singular JSON attachment operations:
`POST /attachments`, `PUT /attachments/:id`, and `DELETE /attachments/:id`.
Each has a preview/execute pair, remains medium sensitivity, and maps only the
`attachments` response root. The frozen scope excludes attachment bulk
operations, binary `POST /files`, `api.files.create`, all UI work, and live
qualification. The opaque attachment payload prevents an invented owner
reference serialisation; the only clear mutable field is `priority`.

The product scope is directory-granular. Its `NODE.md` must restate the exact
file ownership from step 4 and forbid changes to every other file in those
directories; that file-level boundary avoids unrelated implementation drift.

The contract and review leaves are sequential because independence requires the
reviewer to see the committed contract. The product leaf is likewise blocked on
the reviewed contract. No parallel leaf is justified in this slice: the
secondary sales-tax and tertiary bank-line cohorts are explicitly deferred by
the cited brief, and starting them would broaden the freeze before the primary
three rows have an accepted contract.
