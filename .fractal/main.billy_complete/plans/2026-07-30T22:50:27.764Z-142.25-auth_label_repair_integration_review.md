merge, and verification are one ordered integration boundary, and the mandatory
product review needs those exact merged bytes. After the parent validation
passes, create one child with an explicit `--agent=grok` route for the
independent product review. Its ownership is one non-sensitive project-wiki
review record only; it must not modify product code, coverage, node seed, or
scratch evidence. If Grok fails before edits because of authentication, quota,
or rate limiting, retry the same inspection with `--agent=codex-power` as a
supplemental review and leave the mandatory Grok gate open.
