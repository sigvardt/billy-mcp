## Design choices

1. **TDD first (`728BD2E4`).** Confirm honesty-16 still length 16
   and red. First product edit is a failing unit fixture. An empty
   dump must fail `missing_keys` with the exact research section 5
   set. Run it. Capture the failure. Then implement the helper.
2. **Honesty-16 stays red.** Do not remap invoice tools in the
   dump-only half.
3. **Tagged customer first.** Create `MCP-UI-INV-` + 8 hex through
   FastMCP. Confirm the name in a fresh session. Then open
   `/:org_slug/invoices/new` through `ui_invoices_create_open`.
4. **Rest counts before the click.** Expect validation copy 0 and
   **Opret ny** 0. Then one exact **Gem som kladde** click. No
   type. No picker click.
5. **Exact labels only.** Count **Dette felt skal udfyldes.**,
   **Ingen kontakter fundet.**, **Opret ny**, and **Gem som
   kladde** via exact role or exact text. No substring. No body
   sweep.
6. **Invoice must not persist.** If the list or heading becomes
   an invoice detail or list row, set `invoice_persisted=true`,
   `proved_bind=none`, delete the draft, prove absence.
7. **Do not click Opret ny.** Count it. Shot 3 is create-new, not
   the required existing bind.
8. **One option click only when unique tagged match.** Persist
   `proved_bind` only. Do not write the name.
9. **Default stop is `UI_CHANGED`.** If validation copy is
   missing and the list stays closed: do not fall back to closed
   picker probes. Report the owner question from research
   section 5. Do not freeze invoice CUD as done.
10. **CUD is a second half of the same EXECUTE.** Only if
    `proved_bind=draft_save_validation_existing_option`. Smallest
    route means reuse the proved validation-open option. Do not
    fall back to typed `bind_kunde`.
11. **Vision.** Live test may write `author=live_test` and
    `reviewer_verdict=pending_review` only.
12. **No remap** until independent accept + purge of a real CUD.
