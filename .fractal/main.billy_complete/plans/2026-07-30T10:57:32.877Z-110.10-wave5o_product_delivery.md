## Objective

Deliver the narrowly accepted offline API product slice for
`api.invoiceReminders.create`: exactly the preview and execute tools for a
singular invoice-reminder create. Keep every unsupported, unqualified, live,
UI, vision, and bulk claim red. In parallel, obtain a current cited Grok brief
for the next candidate write areas without coupling it to this product change.

## Delegation decision

The product implementation and a current research brief are independent and
have disjoint ownership, so they run as two focused leaves after committing the
root baseline:

1. `wave5o_invoice_reminder_product` is a Codex Power leaf. It owns only
   `src/billy_mcp/api/invoice_reminder_writes.py`, `src/billy_mcp/server.py`,
   `tests/api/test_invoice_reminder_writes.py`,
   `tests/unit/test_coverage_server.py`, `coverage/api_v2_manifest.yaml`,
   `coverage/status.json`, `coverage/report.md`, and its own `.fractal/` seed.
   It may not modify the accepted freeze/review records or any other product
   module.
2. `wave5p_next_write_research` is a Grok leaf. It owns only
   `wiki/wave_fivep_candidate_write_research.md`, the generated corresponding
   `wiki/_index.md` entry, and its own `.fractal/` seed. It must examine current
   official evidence for organizations, users, salesTaxReturns, and
   invoiceReminderAssociations, but may not write source, tests, coverage, or
   make a product/completeness claim.

The independent Wave-5o product review is necessarily sequenced after the
product child produces a committed diff. It will be a fresh Grok leaf after
the root merges the product; if Grok fails before any edit solely due to
authentication, quota, or rate limiting, a Codex Power reviewer repeats the
full review.
