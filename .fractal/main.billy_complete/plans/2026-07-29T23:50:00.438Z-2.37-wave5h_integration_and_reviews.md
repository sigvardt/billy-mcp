## Delegation decision

Two children are warranted because the product review and the freeze research
review require independent external-document judgement, have different source
sets, and write different review records. Root integration is kept local: it
is a short, ordered merge and verification task that must precede the product
review. No Codex Power child is spawned because no fresh implementation work is
authorised until the review gates resolve.
