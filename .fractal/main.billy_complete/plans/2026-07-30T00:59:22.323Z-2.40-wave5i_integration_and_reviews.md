## Independent Grok review fan-out

After the verified root baseline is pushed, start these child nodes in parallel
with `--agent=grok`, `--max-depth=0`, `--max-children=0`, and
`--max-descendants=0`:

1. `wave5i_product_grok_review` — owns only
   `wiki/wave_fivei_product_independent_review.md` plus its own node state.
   Independently re-check current official Billy documentation, the actual root
   merge diff, coverage rows, tool schemas, confirmation binding/replay
   boundaries, and clean-checkout verification. It must issue ACCEPT or REJECT
   for the offline product only, cite its evidence, and keep live/UI/vision/bulk
   and overall completeness fail-closed. It must not modify product code or
   coverage values.
2. `wave5j_freeze_research_grok_review` — owns only
   `wiki/wave_fivej_freeze_ready_research_independent_review.md` plus its own
   node state. It must independently verify the current official documents and
   the nine bank-line singular CUD claims; explicitly test the 401/405/empty
   DELETE interpretation, opaque enum/relationship boundaries, optional match
   response roots, and exclusion of all bulk and bank-payment delete work. It
   may ACCEPT or REJECT the research handoff only; it must not author the freeze
   contract, product tools, or coverage greens.

Use explicit bounded review-node configuration at spawn: `--max-cost=160`,
`--max-iter-cost=40`, `--max-iters=6`, `--step-timeout=30m`, and
`--timeout=3h`. Configure each node before launch with its exclusive file
ownership and requirement to report an immutable commit tip and durable verdict
while still running.

## Follow-up gate

Monitor both reviews, merge only their durable review pages after checking their
committed diffs and verdicts, and update root state. An accepted Wave-5i audit
opens only the next freeze sequence; a rejected audit routes the precise defect
to a new Codex Power repair leaf and then a fresh Grok review. An accepted
Wave-5j research audit opens only the cited freeze-page authoring gate; it does
not authorise bank-line product tools or coverage changes.
