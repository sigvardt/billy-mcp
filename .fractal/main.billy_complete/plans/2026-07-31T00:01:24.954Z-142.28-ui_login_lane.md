manager iteration rather than an inline implementation:

1. Parent integration owns only the already-completed
   `main.billy_complete.wave5u_research96_harness` handoff at `6099f20`.
   Its product diff must remain limited to
   `src/billy_mcp/live_probe.py` and `tests/unit/test_live_probe.py`; the
   child seed is audit metadata, never evidence of a product-scope expansion.
2. A focused `grok` leaf owns a fresh, cited UI login-surface contract. It has
   a clean research context and cannot accidentally implement a tool. It will
   decide whether the observed unauthenticated login surface supports a
   distinct, non-generic interface-only read workflow or whether the first

   product leaf implement the resulting bounded source/test/coverage contract.
   This sequencing prevents an invented `ui_*` wrapper or unobserved
   post-login selector from reaching the server.
