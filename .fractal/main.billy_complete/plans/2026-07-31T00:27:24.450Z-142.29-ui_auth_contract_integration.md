## Decision and evidence

The cited Grok research98 relay in `.fractal/main.billy_complete/tmp/grok-research.md`
and the child brief at `08165b6:wiki/ui_login_surface_contract.md` agree on a
single safe decision: the observed credential-absent page supports no new
`ui_*` workflow. It is already the fixed, typed `auth_status` state. A new

business UI workflow all require a dedicated non-production browser session,
a named non-production organisation, and (where read-back is required) a valid
company API credential. Live and vision coverage stays red.

## Delegation decision

No product child is spawned in this slice. The evidence expressly rejects a
duplicate `ui_*` login product, and every remaining shared-auth requirement
depends on unobserved authenticated DOM. A code leaf now would either invent a
contract or be a no-op, so it cannot satisfy the product acceptance bar.

When dedicated non-production credentials are available, the next split is:

1. A `grok` discovery leaf, with exclusive ownership of a cited shared wiki
   brief, observes one headless credentialed transition and records the exact
   authenticated state, selectors, API read-back, challenge handling, and
   evidence purge.
2. Only after that brief is merged, a `codex-power` leaf owns the disjoint
   typed shared-auth implementation, offline/real headless tests, and red
   coverage-state preservation. A separate `grok` review then evaluates the
   merged product and visual evidence.

Residual and ambiguous-bulk work remains excluded by the operator directive.
