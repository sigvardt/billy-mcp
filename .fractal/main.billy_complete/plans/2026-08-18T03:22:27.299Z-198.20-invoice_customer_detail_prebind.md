   and red. First product edit is a failing unit fixture. An empty
   dump must fail `missing_keys` with the exact research section 5
   set. Run it. Capture the failure. Then implement the helper.

   dump-only half.

   `ui_clients_get_open`.

   path to `invoices_new` / `invoices_new_with_query` / `other` /

   or invent `?contact=` before a live href proves it.

   is safe. Do not click **Gem som kladde** until prebind is
   proved and a failing product fixture exists.
7. **Default stop is `UI_CHANGED`.** If `exact_opret_faktura_count`
   is 0: do not click, delete the customer, prove absence, close

8. **Prebind CUD is a second half of the same EXECUTE.** Only if
   `proved_prebind=customer_detail_opret_faktura`. Smallest route
   means reuse the proved role or href. Do not fall back to
   `bind_kunde` on `/invoices/new`.
9. **Vision.** Live test may write `author=live_test` and
   `reviewer_verdict=pending_review` only.
10. **No remap** until independent accept + purge of a real CUD.
