## Delegation decision

The freeze review is an independent current-document task and therefore belongs
to Grok. The two product modules are independent after an accepted frozen
contract, so they belong to separate Codex Power leaves. Root retains shared
registration, generator, coverage, and cross-module safety tests to avoid
conflicting ownership. No leaf is started before the freeze gate is accepted.
