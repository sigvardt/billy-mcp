# 110.14 Organizations integration and users freeze gate

## Decision

Treat the completed Wave-5p organizations child as a reviewable product
candidate, not as accepted work. Its committed `fbc8996` slice is independent
from the newly written Wave-5q users research package, so the parent can
integrate and commission the product review while a separate Codex Power leaf
authors the users **wiki-only** freeze page. The two leaves own distinct
surfaces and neither may advance live, UI, vision, bulk, or completeness status.

The current root has Research70's uncommitted durable research artifacts. They
must be preserved and committed before any new child starts, since child nodes
fork committed bytes only. Research70 is a Grok cited brief; it is sufficient
evidence for a Codex Power freeze-page authoring leaf, but it is not freeze
ACCEPT or authority for users product code.

   `git merge --no-ff -m "merge main.billy_complete.wave5p_organizations_product" main.billy_complete.wave5p_organizations_product`.
   Regenerate coverage, confirm 260 registered `api_*` tools and exactly 177

   state into private memory and commit a clean baseline before launching
