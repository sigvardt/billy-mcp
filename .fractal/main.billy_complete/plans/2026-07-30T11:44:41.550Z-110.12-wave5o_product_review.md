## Child contract

In EXECUTE, configure and launch a leaf with the explicit Grok route:

```text
fractal node init wave5o_product_review --path=/Volumes/ssd_1/Repositories/billy-mcp/.worktrees/main.billy_complete --agent=grok --max-cost=320 --max-iter-cost=80 --max-iters=8 --step-timeout=20m --max-depth=0 --max-children=0 --max-descendants=0
```

The child owns only `wiki/wave_fiveo_product_independent_review.md`, its
generated project-wiki index row, and its own seed. It must not modify source,
tests, coverage, status, or prior contracts. It must independently check the
merged bytes, typed flat schemas, no-network preview, exact single POST/no
retry, one-use ticket binding, `invoiceReminders`-only success mapping,
server registration, focused tests, generator output, and coverage honesty.
The review must cite current official evidence, record `ACCEPT` or concrete
defects, and explicitly leave live, UI, vision, cleanup qualification, bulk,
webhooks, and completeness unclaimed. If Grok fails before edits solely from
authentication, quota, or rate limiting, retry the entire review with
`--agent=codex-power`; never switch after edits.

## Parent follow-through

After the child commits, inspect its three-dot diff and merge only the review
record after preserving the reviewed scope. Run wiki lint and root non-live
verification before treating any ACCEPT as authoritative. The review never
unblocks organizations implementation; that remains gated on the separate
Wave-5p freeze review.
